# Executive Summary

The UAC care pipeline requires daily visibility into how many children are entering custody, transferring into HHS care, remaining under care, and being discharged to vetted sponsors. This project provides a centralized analytics framework and Streamlit dashboard to monitor that system in near real time.

## What the Dashboard Shows

- Total children under care across CBP and HHS.
- Balance between HHS inflow and sponsor-placement outflow.
- Backlog accumulation when transfers exceed discharges.
- Volatility and sustained strain windows.
- Data-quality warnings for missing dates, duplicate dates, and logical inconsistencies.

## Why It Matters

Operational leaders need to know not only the current census, but whether pressure is increasing or easing. A rising net intake trend can signal future shelter, clinical, case-management, and sponsor-vetting stress before it becomes visible as overcrowding.

## Recommended Use

Use the dashboard as a recurring situational-awareness tool for capacity planning, healthcare staffing, shelter management, and policy briefings. Replace the sample data with official daily data before making operational decisions.

## Core Recommendation

Track flow balance and stress windows alongside total system load. These indicators provide earlier warning of strain than headline custody counts alone.
